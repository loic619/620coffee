# CFTC

Commitments of Traders (COT) positioning reports published by the U.S. Commodity
Futures Trading Commission.

- **Upstream:** https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/
- **Cadence:** weekly
- **Scope:** the reported positioning series as published. Public domain as a
  work of the U.S. federal government.
- **Processing:** parsed into JSON with normalised field names and ISO 8601
  dates. Reported figures are unchanged.

Datasets in this directory are indexed in `/catalog.json` under source `cftc`.
